Droids meet you wherever you work.

Droids embed directly into your workflow. IDE, Web, CLI, Slack, Linear. Delegate tasks as they come to mind, wherever you are.

Droids where you code

Factory meets you in your editor without forcing a switch. Delegate to Droids in any IDE or terminal: VS Code, JetBrains, Vim, and more. All while maintaining your workflow, tools, and shortcuts.

Droids in the browser

Run Droids directly from the web. Developers can delegate complex coding tasks, refactors, or debugging to agents instantly—no setup required. It’s the fastest way to experience agent-driven development, with a UI designed for clarity and speed.

Droids at scale

Script and parallelize Droids at massive scale for CI/CD, migrations, and maintenance. From automated code review to self-healing builds, integrate agents into every phase of development.

Droids in the war room

Give support teams and engineers a shared line to Droids. From incident triage to small bug fixes, anyone can delegate tasks in plain English and get code-level solutions. Faster fixes, lower MTTR, and broader adoption across the org.
